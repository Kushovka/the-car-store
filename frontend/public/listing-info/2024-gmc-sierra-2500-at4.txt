Exterior and InteriorWearing a white coat, you’ll immediately notice that this 2024 GMC Sierra 2500 HD Crew Cab sits taller than stock. That’s because it’s got a 6.5-inch lift with BDS adjustable coilovers plus front and rear FOX shocks. Moreover, the lift is needed to amply accommodate the 35-inch Toyo R/T tires on 22-inch Foreplay aftermarket wheels. You’ve also got the AT4 Premium Plus Package, Preferred Package, a power-sliding rear window and even the Gooseneck and Fifth Wheel Package. The cargo bed features stamped holes with caps for better cargo securing, as well as a 7-pin trailer harness. Meanwhile, the passenger cabin enjoys tinted windows, and the cargo bed is protected via a tonneau cover. Yup, you can use this truck like an SUV if you desire.
The interior of this 2024 GMC Sierra 2500 HD Crew Cab comes with gray leather upholstery and can easily accommodate five full-sized adults. In fact, if we take the cabin’s interior volume in a purely academic sense, it’s roomier than many full-size luxury sedans! Plus, you’ve got features such as adaptive cruise control, the Technology Package, a multi-color heads-up display, dual-zone climate control and even a power sunroof. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are standard, as is a Bose sound system too.EngineThis 2024 GMC Sierra 2500 HD Crew Cab packs a 6.6-liter Duramax turbodiesel V8 under the hood. It’s rated to reportedly make 470hp and 975lb-ft, which are staggering figures, to say the least. Power is sent to all four wheels through a 10-speed automatic gearbox from Allison.
