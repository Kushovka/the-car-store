Description:
Exterior and Interior
The Cybertruck is renowned for its 300-series shiny silver stainless steel body panels, much like another car from a few decades back, the iconic DeLorean. While stainless steel is a notoriously difficult color to paint, you’ve now got the option of wraps. That’s what this 2024 Tesla Cybertruck Foundation Series wears: an Army Green wrap, giving it a distinctive appearance from the majority of others on the road. But if Army Green isn’t your thing, you can wrap it in another color of choice or remove the wrap and showcase the truck’s stainless steel bodywork. Either way, its futuristic, angular design and distinctive wheel caps make for a stunning machine.
The interior of this 2024 Tesla Cybertruck Foundation Series features a simplistic dashboard dominated by a single screen. However, don’t be fooled into thinking that simple means spartan. Nope, the truck has all the tech features you’d expect from a Tesla. It’s just controlled from this screen. Take a closer look, and you’ll see items such as power-adjustable and heated front seats, dual-zone automatic climate control, a full infotainment system, and more. Plus, you’ve got some cool 3D all-weather liners, plus special laser-etched Foundation Series badges and cabin graphics. As for cargo capacity, there’s a fully enclosed truck bed plus a front trunk. With a 2,500lb payload capacity, you’ve got something akin to a full-size pickup truck right here, replete with a motorized roller tonneau for safety and security. Furthermore, the towing capacity is an impressive 11,000lbs.

Engine
This 2024 Tesla Cybertruck Foundation Series comes with, as you’d expect, electric propulsion. With a dual-motor configuration, you’ll enjoy 600hp and 743lb-ft at your disposal. Furthermore, the truck comes with full Tesla Self-Drive capabilities.
