Exterior and InteriorThe 2020 BMW M760i drive is a perfect blend of fly-under-the-radar
stealth along with its head-turning presence. This particular example

facelifted 7-Series with a more modern exterior and interior. With an
original MSRP of over $170,000, this car is loaded with a myriad of
options. It features Icon Adaptive LEDs with Laser light, extended
Shadow Line trim, carbon fiber mirror caps, painted rear spoiler, and
floating hubcaps are all part of an extensive exterior options list.
This example is an M-Sport, which means aggressive bumpers and side
sills along with upgraded brakes sitting behind 20-inch M-Sport
wheels. The car is finished in a gorgeous deep Bernina grey amber
effect paint with blacked-out accents.
As you step in, LED door projectors with the M Sport projection greet
you. The black Nappa leather interior offers electric seats all around
to ensure all passengers ride in supreme comfort. The individual piano
black trim, along with a panoramic sky lounge LED roof, exudes
sophistication and class. The Bowers and Wilkins sound system provides
state-of-the-art surround sound, giving an immersive audio experience.EngineThe best thing about BMW is even their luxury limousines are insanely
fast and fun to drive. With the M-Sport tuning on top it is even more
aggressive. Powering the M760i is a 6.6-liter twin-turbocharged V12
that transfers 600 hp and 800 Nm of torque to all four wheels through
a smooth eight-speed automatic transmission. It sprints to 60 mph in
just 3.5 seconds, which is impressive for a 6,000 lb luxury limousine.
