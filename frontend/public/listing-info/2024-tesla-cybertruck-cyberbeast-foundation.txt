Description:
Exterior and Interior
The 2024 Tesla Cybertruck Cyberbeast Foundation Series is nothing like
any other pickup truck, even the most modern electric ones from other
brands. The ultra-hard stainless steel exoskeleton of the Cybertruck
is unpainted and finished in a Silver/Steel finish. The windshield and
windows of the truck comprise armored shatter-resistant glass,
ensuring superior protection and enhanced noise isolation. Adding to
the rugged appeal of the Cyberbeast is a set of 20-inch Cyber wheels
encircled by 35-inch Goodyear Wrangler Territory RT tires. At the rear
end, there is a 6x4 feet lockable cargo bed, whereas there is a frunk
upfront offering extra storage space. However, it is to be noted that
the standard light bar and rear tow hooks of this particular truck
have not arrived yet. On the inside, there is ample space for five
occupants and the cabin is specced in a simple and minimalistic Black
and White color combo. Technology is abundant inside this 2024 Tesla
Cybertruck Cyberbeast Foundation Series. There is a massive,
dashboard-mounted 8.5-inch central touchscreen display, a 9.4-inch
touchscreen at the rear, and a 15-speaker audio system.

Engine
Propelling the 2024 Tesla Cybertruck Cyberbeast Foundation Series is a
tri-motor electric powertrain, comprising one 276 hp motor on the
front axle and two 284 hp rear-axle-mounted motors. Powering these
motors is a 123 kWh liquid-cooled lithium-ion battery pack, capable of
a peak charging rate of 250 kW. The combined output generated by this
setup is a whopping 845 horsepower and 740 lb-ft, sent to all four
wheels via a direct-drive transmission. The 6,843 lbs Cyberbeast can
gallop to 60 mph in just 2.6 seconds and is rated to tow up to 11,000
lbs. Tesla promises an estimated range of 320 miles and has included
an 11.5 kW charger onboard. Furthermore, being the Foundation Series
edition, the truck is also equipped with Tesla’s Powershare home
backup hardware and full self-driving capability. Overall, this
outlandish badass electric pickup truck is supersonic on the road and
impressively competent off it. It is also exceptionally tech-loaded,
with plenty of space for people and luggage.
