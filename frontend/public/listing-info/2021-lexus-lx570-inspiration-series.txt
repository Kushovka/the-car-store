Description:
Exterior and InteriorFinished in Black Onyx, this 2021 Lexus LX 570 Inspiration Series carries an unmistakably stealthy presence. The Inspiration Series package adds a host of exclusive black exterior details, including black 21-inch wheels, black grille accents, smoked lighting elements, black roof rails, black chrome trim, and exclusive badging that give the traditionally elegant LX a much more aggressive and contemporary personality. Despite its premium presentation, the LX remains highly functional, retaining its full-size SUV practicality, commanding ride height, and genuine off-road capability beneath the luxury veneer.

Inside, the cabin reflects Lexus’ signature approach to craftsmanship and comfort. The exclusive black and Rioja Red-accented semi-aniline leather interior immediately distinguishes this Inspiration Series from ordinary LX 570s, while the Luxury Package adds heated and ventilated front and second-row outboard seating, four-zone climate control, and premium trim materials throughout. Convenience and technology features include a head-up display, wireless charging, cool box, heated espresso wood-and-leather steering wheel, and the superb Mark Levinson 19-speaker, 450-watt Reference Surround Sound System. Even by modern standards, the cabin remains exceptionally comfortable, solidly built, and unmistakably upscale.EngineAt the heart of this 2021 Lexus LX 570 Inspiration Series sits Lexus’ venerable 5.7-liter naturally aspirated V8, producing smooth, effortless power delivery that prioritizes refinement and durability over forced-induction theatrics. Paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and a full-time all-wheel-drive system, the LX delivers confident all-weather performance while retaining the serious off-road DNA inherited from its Land Cruiser platform sibling. Advanced systems such as adaptive variable suspension, active height control, crawl control, and multi-terrain select make this far more than just a luxury cruiser—it remains one of the most capable full-size SUVs ever engineered. What makes the LX 570 especially compelling today is its analog honesty: a naturally aspirated V8, body-on-frame construction, and proven mechanical engineering in a segment increasingly dominated by more complex alternatives.
