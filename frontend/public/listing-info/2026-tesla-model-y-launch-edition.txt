Exterior and Interior
This 2026 Tesla Model Y Launch Edition wears a Pearl White coat of paint and rolls on a set of 19-inch aerodynamically designed alloy wheels. These are shod in Continental ProContact tires for optimal grip and performance, given that performance is a key attribute of Tesla’s vehicles. In fact, the overall design of the Model Y, like other Teslas, screams effortless performance. The aerodynamic design, futuristic lines and overall space-age presence mean that you can never mistake a Tesla for anything else – be it American, European or Far Eastern.
The sleek black interior features white seats and can accommodate five adults with ease. It may look highly minimalistic at first glance but looks are indeed deceiving. There’s so much tech on board, backed by a ton of processing power. In fact, you can engage in console-level gaming via the car’s systems, such is the computing prowess available. OF course, you’ve got the usual dual-zone automatic climate control, power front seats, smartphone connectivity and the essentials of modern automotive amenities at your fingertips. Rear-seat passengers get their own touchscreen for adjusting settings too. And for cargo, there are two spaces! Yes, the regular trunk out back is there, plus a front trunk, or frunk for even more capacity.

Engine
This 2026 Tesla Model Y Launch Edition comes with dual electric motors. Hence, it’s all-wheel drive. With a reported total of 384hp and 376lb-ft available, 0-60 takes just under 5 seconds on average. An 81 kilowatt-hour battery pack ensures ample range, and of course, Tesla’s famed Supercharger network is available for you to use.
