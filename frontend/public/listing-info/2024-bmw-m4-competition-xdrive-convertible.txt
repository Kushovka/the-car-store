Description:
Exterior and InteriorOriginally, this 2024 BMW M4 Competition came in Black Sapphire Metallic. That paint job is still there, beneath the matte black vinyl wrap that this car currently wears. So, the choice is yours – metallic paint or matte effect. Regardless of your choice, the black powered soft top complements the car’s looks, as do the 826M Bi-Color wheels that are 19-inches at the front and 20-inches at the rear. You’ve also got M Shadowline lights, as well as the red calipers of those M Compound upgraded brakes. Quad tailpipes out back aren’t for show either, they really let out a spine-tingling soundtrack when you drive this car hard.
The black interior of this 2024 BMW M4 Competition can seat four occupants and comes with the Extended Merino Leather specification. It’s your typical BMW driver-focused cabin with a digital gauge cluster, BMW’s iDrive infotainment system, and paddle shifters on the multifunction steering wheel. You’ll also find a nice Harman/Kardon sound system that gels well with your choice of tunes, ideally piped via Apple CarPlay or Android Auto. Other features include climate control, powered and heated front seats, charging ports, cup holders and all the usual refinements you’d expect from a BMW. You’ve also got a Drive Recorder and M Performance floor mats.EngineThis 2024 BMW M4 Competition features a twin-turbocharged 3.0-liter straight six. The engine is tuned to deliver 503hp and 479lb-ft. It’s twinned with an ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox and drives all four wheels. Thanks to the all-wheel drive system, a low 3-second 0-60 time is very doable.
