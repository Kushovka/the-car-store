Description:
Exterior and Interior
The Model Y is a compact-class crossover SUV, and is heavily based on the Model 3 compact car. This particular 2025 Tesla Model Y Performance wears a Deep Blue Metallic paint job, and rides on a suitably alluring set of 21-inch Uberturbine wheels. Red brake calipers lie behind each wheel, peeking out behind the spokes. Then, the vehicle’s exterior LED lighting shines bright when called upon, while its sleek, coupe-like stance gives it a real performance-car vibe. The interior of this 2025 Tesla Model Y Performance is minimalistic in form, comprehensive in function. You’ve got Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, as well as a super-powerful infotainment system that can easily run a myriad of video games. Then, there’s powered and heated seats, climate control with air conditioning, a decent speaker system, and even Tesla’s famed Autopilot autonomous driving system. Now we must tell you that this is to be used responsibly; don’t engage it and climb into the passenger or rear seats. Instead, consider it an advanced form of cruise control, meant to relax you as much as possible on long drives – but you must remain at the wheel, and alert at all times. As for trunk space, you’ve got the usual rear trunk behind the rear seats, plus a front storage compartment, called a Frunk. When the rear seat backs are dropped and both compartments are combined, you’ll have over 75 cubic feet of total cargo capacity.

Engine
Powering this 2025 Tesla Model Y Performance is a duo of electric motors that drive all four wheels and make up to a reported 456hp and 497lb-ft of torque. The vehicle’s long-range battery can be charged at a regular home charger, or a fast charging facility such as Tesla’s Supercharger network."
